The Context Stack

// Level 1 of 7 · Stop re-explaining yourself to AI every Monday.
The problem: You open Claude. You paste the project brief again. You explain where you left off again. Fifteen minutes in, you're finally ready to work. Longer prompts are a treadmill. The fix is structure: 5 layers, 60-second load, 10-minute weekly reset.
// THE_STACK
01
Identity
static
Who you are, your role, your non-negotiables. Things that don't change week-to-week.
identity.md
02
Projects
semi-static
One brief per active project. Goals, stakeholders, stack, decisions. Updated weekly.
project-*.md
03
State
dynamic · daily
What you touched today. What's blocked. What's next. Your working memory.
state.md
04
Artifacts
referenced
Code, docs, past decisions — linked, not copy-pasted. The moment you paste, it's stale.
link, don't copy
05
Patterns
reusable
Prompt templates, rubrics, rules that worked. Saved, not re-invented.
patterns/
// THE_DRILLS

Daily Load

~ 60 sec
  1. Open your AI tool.
  2. Reference identity.md + the relevant project-*.md + state.md.
  3. Ask: "Load these. Then tell me where I left off."

Monday Reset

~ 10 min
  1. Update each project file with last week's decisions.
  2. Archive last week's state.md. Start a fresh one.
  3. Prune patterns/ — delete anything unused in 30 days.
// THE_MATH Before: 15 min × 5 sessions = 75 min/wk on the treadmill. After: 60 sec × 5 + 10 min reset = 15 min/wk. Recovered: ~1 hr/wk per person → 10 hrs/wk across a team of 10.
